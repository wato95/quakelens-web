## U.S. Geological Survey earthquake data

Earthquake data used by QuakeLens are sourced from the U.S. Geological Survey
(USGS).

USGS states that USGS-authored or produced data and information are considered
to be in the U.S. public domain, while noting that some material published on
USGS websites may originate from third parties and remain copyrighted.

USGS requests appropriate credit when its information products, publications,
or website information are reused.

QuakeLens therefore attributes earthquake data to:

> U.S. Geological Survey (USGS)

USGS copyright and credit guidance:

https://www.usgs.gov/information-policies-and-instructions/copyrights-and-credits

The QuakeLens software licence does not apply to USGS data.

## OpenFreeMap

The default QuakeLens basemap is provided through OpenFreeMap.

OpenFreeMap's project code is MIT licensed. Its hosted map stack uses
OpenMapTiles and map data from OpenStreetMap.

OpenFreeMap:
https://openfreemap.org/

OpenFreeMap attribution guidance:
https://openfreemap.org/

QuakeLens retains the attribution supplied with the map style.

## OpenMapTiles

The basemap schema and styles used through OpenFreeMap are based on
OpenMapTiles.

OpenMapTiles requires attribution when its maps are displayed.

Project:
https://openmaptiles.org/

QuakeLens displays OpenMapTiles attribution as part of the map attribution
control.

## OpenStreetMap

Basemap geographic data are derived from OpenStreetMap.

OpenStreetMap data are licensed under the Open Data Commons Open Database
License (ODbL).

Copyright and licence information:
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

QuakeLens retains visible attribution to OpenStreetMap contributors in the
interactive map.

## Map attribution

The default interactive map displays attribution equivalent to:

> OpenFreeMap © OpenMapTiles · Data from OpenStreetMap

Attribution must not be removed from deployed QuakeLens maps.

For screenshots, videos, documentation, or other static media containing the
map, the relevant map attribution should remain visible in the image or be
provided immediately alongside it.
